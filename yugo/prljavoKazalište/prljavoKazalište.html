About Prljavo Kazalište

Prljavo Kazalište is a legendary rock band from Zagreb, formed in 1977. They started as a punk band but quickly evolved into a mainstream rock act, becoming one of the most popular groups in the former Yugoslavia. Their greatest success came in the late 1980s and early 1990s with iconic albums and songs such as Kiše jesenje, Ne zovi mama doktora, and Uzalud vam trud svirači, which remain some of their most celebrated and influential works.

After internal disagreements, the band eventually split into two parallel versions of Prljavo Kazalište. One is led by Jasenko Houra, while the other is fronted by original singer Davorin Bogović. Today, both versions continue to perform and preserve the band’s legacy, each interpreting its classic songs in its own way.
