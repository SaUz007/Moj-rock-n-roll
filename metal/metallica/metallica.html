About Metallica

Metallica is one of the most influential heavy metal bands in the world. Formed in 1981 in Los Angeles by James Hetfield and Lars Ulrich, the band became a leading force in the development of thrash metal. Their fast riffs, powerful vocals, and complex songwriting helped shape modern metal music.

Metallica gained global fame with albums like Master of Puppets (1986) and Metallica (1991). Songs such as Enter Sandman and Nothing Else Matters became worldwide hits.

Over the decades, Metallica has sold millions of albums, won multiple Grammy Awards, and remains one of the most successful and respected bands in heavy metal history.

Current Members

  • James Hetfield (1981–) – Vocals, Rhythm Guitar
  • Kirk Hammet (1983–) – Lead Guitar
  • Robert Trujillo (2003–) – Bass Guitar
  • Lars Ulrich (1981–) – Drums

Past Members

  • Dave Mustaine (1981–1983) – Rhythm Guitar
  • Cliff Burton (1982–1986) – Bass Guitar
  • Jason Newsted (1986–2001) – Bass Guitar
  • Ron McGovney (1981–1982) – Bass Guitar
